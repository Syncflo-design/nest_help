How to Close a POS Shift

1 Click POS Closing from the Nest Home dashboard.
2 Select the POS Profile (the store) and the User whose shift you are closing.
3 The system automatically pulls in all POS invoices for that shift. Review the listed transactions.
4 In the payment reconciliation section, enter the actual amounts counted for each payment method (cash, card, etc.).
5 Click Submit to close the shift. The system records any differences between expected and actual amounts.
Always count your cash drawer and card totals before creating the closing entry. Discrepancies are logged and visible to managers.
