Financial Reports

NestERP Help

What This Does

Financial Reports give you a complete picture of your company's financial health. Access the Profit and Loss statement to review revenue and expenses, the Balance Sheet for assets, liabilities, and equity, and the General Ledger for detailed transaction history. Use Trial Balance to verify account totals, and review Accounts Receivable and Accounts Payable reports to monitor outstanding customer and supplier balances.

How to Get Here

1 Click the Financial Reports tile on your home page or navigate to /app/accounting.
2 The workspace opens with shortcuts to Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet, General Ledger, Trial Balance, Accounts Receivable, and Accounts Payable.
You can jump to any financial report instantly by pressing Ctrl+K and typing its name.
