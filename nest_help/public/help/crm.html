CRM Workspace

NestERP Help

What This Does

The CRM workspace helps you manage customer relationships and track your sales pipeline from first contact to closed deal. Capture new prospects as Leads, qualify them into Opportunities with expected revenue and close dates, and log all customer communication in one place. Use the pipeline view to monitor deal stages, identify bottlenecks, and forecast upcoming revenue across your sales team.

How to Get Here

1 Click the CRM tile on your home page or navigate to /app/crm.
2 The CRM workspace opens with shortcuts to Leads, Opportunities, and sales pipeline tracking tools.
You can jump to any CRM record instantly by pressing Ctrl+K and typing its name.
