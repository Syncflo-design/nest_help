NestERP Help

What This Does

The Settings area is where you configure the core behavior of your NestERP system. Update Company details like address and logo, set System Defaults for currency, fiscal year, and date format, configure Email Settings for notifications and outgoing mail, customize Print Formats for invoices and reports, manage Naming Series for document numbering, and adjust global configuration options that affect every module.

How to Get Here

1 Click the Settings tile on your home page or navigate to /app/settings.
2 The Settings workspace opens with shortcuts to Company, System Settings, Email, Print Formats, Naming Series, and other global configuration options.
You can jump to any settings page instantly by pressing Ctrl+K and typing its name.
