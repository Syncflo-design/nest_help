Manufacturing Workspace

NestERP Help

What This Does

The Manufacturing workspace manages your production processes from planning through completion. Define product recipes with Bills of Materials (BOMs), schedule production runs using Work Orders, and track shop-floor progress through Job Cards. Production reports give you visibility into output rates, material consumption, and work-in-progress so you can optimize throughput and minimize waste.

How to Get Here

1 Click the Manufacturing tile on your home page or navigate to /app/manufacturing.
2 The Manufacturing workspace opens with shortcuts to BOMs, Work Orders, Job Cards, and production reports.
You can jump to any manufacturing document instantly by pressing Ctrl+K and typing its name.
