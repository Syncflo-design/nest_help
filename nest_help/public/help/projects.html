Projects Workspace

NestERP Help

What This Does

The Projects workspace organizes work into trackable deliverables. Create Projects with start and end dates, break them into Tasks assigned to team members, and log hours with Time Tracking. Visualize timelines on Gantt Charts or manage workflow stages using Kanban Boards to keep every project on schedule and on budget.

How to Get Here

1 Click the Projects tile on your home page or navigate to /app/projects.
2 The Projects workspace opens with shortcuts to Projects, Tasks, Time Tracking, Gantt Charts, and Kanban Boards.
You can jump to any project or task instantly by pressing Ctrl+K and typing its name.
