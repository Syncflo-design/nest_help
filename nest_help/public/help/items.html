Item List

NestERP Help

What This Does

Opens the full item master list at /app/item. This is the central register of every product in the system.

How to Use It

1 Click Items from the Nest Home dashboard to view all products.
2 Use the search bar and filters to find specific items by name, item group, or item code.
3 Click any item to open it and edit its details — name, selling price, images, item group, and other defaults.
4 To add a new item, click + Add Item at the top of the list (or use the dedicated New Item button on the dashboard).
Changes to item details (like price or name) take effect immediately. If the item is used in active POS sessions, the updated details will appear on the next transaction.
