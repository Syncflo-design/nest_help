Stock Workspace

NestERP Help

What This Does

The Stock workspace provides complete inventory management across all your warehouses. Use Stock Entries to record material receipts, transfers, and consumption. Run Stock Reconciliation to correct quantities after physical counts. Manage your Items and Warehouses, and monitor inventory health through reports like Stock Balance and Stock Ledger for real-time visibility into what you have, where it is, and how it moves.

How to Get Here

1 Click the Stock tile on your home page or navigate to /app/stock.
2 The Stock workspace opens with shortcuts to Stock Entries, Items, Warehouses, Stock Reconciliation, and inventory reports.
You can jump to any stock document instantly by pressing Ctrl+K and typing its name.
