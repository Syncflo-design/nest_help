Adding Artists

Sculptors, Throwers & Painters — NestERP Help

What This Does

Artists — your sculptors, throwers and painters — are stored in the system as Manufacturers. When you import a kiln sheet, each item's sculptor, thrower and painter is linked to a Manufacturer record by matching the artist's Full Name.

If an artist on your kiln sheet does not yet exist in the system, that name will come through blank on the item. So any new artist must be added here before you import a sheet that uses them.

Adding a New Artist

1 From your home screen, click Manufacturers
2 Click + Add Manufacturer (top right)
3 Short Name — a short code for the artist, maximum 12 characters (e.g. Sakhile N). This is the artist's unique ID in the system.
4 Full Name — the artist's full name, spelled exactly as it appears on your kiln sheet (e.g. Sakhile Ntshalintshali)
5 Click Save
The import matches artists on the Full Name. The spelling and spacing must match your kiln sheet exactly — if they differ, the artist will not link and the name will import blank. Add the artist first, then run your import.

Editing an Artist

1 Click Manufacturers, then click the artist's name in the list
2 Update the details and click Save

If you have already imported items with a missing artist, you do not have to re-import. Add the artist here, then open the affected item and select the artist in the Sculptor / Thrower / Painter field directly.
The same Manufacturer record can be used for any role — an artist who both throws and paints only needs one record, and can be selected as the Thrower on one item and the Painter on another.
