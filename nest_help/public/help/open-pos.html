Opening the Till

1 Click Open POS from the Nest Home dashboard.
2 The first time each day the till asks you to open it. Choose your POS Profile (your store), enter the opening amount for each payment type (enter 0 if there is nothing to carry over) and click Submit. This applies to the online store till as well.
3 In the Select POS User dropdown, select your name.
If the opening box appears and you do not want to open the till, click Not now to go back to the home screen (or press F5 to reload the page). If you see a red message Outdated POS Opening Entry, yesterday's till was never closed. Go to POS Closing on the home screen, close the old entry, then click Open POS again.

Making a Sale

1 Find the item. Type part of the name, the item code or the 8-digit item number in the search box, or scan the barcode. Each tile shows the full item name, the item code and item number on one line, and the price at the bottom.
2 Click the tile to add the item to the cart.
3 To change the quantity, click the quantity box on the cart line, type the quantity and press Enter (or click anywhere else). Type 0 to remove the line.
4 Click Checkout.
5 Choose how the customer is paying: Credit Card or Voucher. Credit Card is filled in with the full amount for you.
6 Click Complete Order. The sale is finished straight away and the receipt screen opens. There is no further confirmation.
Items sold by the unit (Each) take whole numbers only. Items sold by the metre take part quantities, for example 2.5 or 2,5. The till refuses a quantity that is more than the stock in your store.

Paying with a Voucher

1 On the payment screen click Voucher and type the voucher amount. Amounts are typed with the cents, so R500.00 is 5 0 0 0 0.
2 If the voucher covers only part of the sale, the rest moves to Credit Card automatically.
3 Click Complete Order. The till asks for the Voucher Reference No. Type the number on the voucher and click Save.

The voucher number is stored on the invoice and printed on the receipt.

Returns and Refunds

1 In the till, click Recent Orders at the top right.
2 Select the original sale from the list. Use the Sales / Returns filter and the search box to find it.
3 Click Return.
4 Tick the items the customer is bringing back, adjust the quantity if only some are returned, and click Return ticked items.
5 Click Checkout, then Complete Order. The refund goes to Credit Card. A refund cannot be paid out as a voucher.

What Happens in Sage

Every sale and every return is sent to Sage as soon as it is completed, and Sage stock is adjusted. You do not have to do anything. If Sage cannot be reached, the sale still goes through on the till and the system sends it again automatically every 15 minutes.

Your name must be added to the store's POS Profile before it appears in the dropdown. If you don't see your name, ask your manager to add you. Close the till with POS Closing at the end of every day.
