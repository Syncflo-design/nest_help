Stock Balance Report

NestERP Help

What This Does

Shows current stock levels across all warehouses. Each row displays the item, warehouse, available quantity, and stock value.

How to Use It

1 Click Stock Balance from the Nest Home dashboard to open the report at /app/query-report/Stock Balance.
2 Use the filters at the top to narrow results:
  • Warehouse — view stock for a specific store or location
  • Item Group — filter by product category
  • Company — filter by company
3 Review the quantities and values. Click any item row to drill down into its stock ledger for a full movement history.
This report reflects real-time stock levels. For a warehouse-specific view that is pre-filtered to your store, use the "Our Items" button instead.
